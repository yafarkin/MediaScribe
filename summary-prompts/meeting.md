MEETING OR WORK SESSION PROFILE

Create an actionable record of the discussion. Include, when supported by the source:
1. The purpose, context and participants, using only supplied names or labels.
2. Decisions made, including the agreed outcome and relevant rationale.
3. Action items, each with the task, owner and deadline only when explicitly stated.
4. Important discussion points, alternatives considered and reasons for accepting or
   rejecting them.
5. Problems, risks, dependencies, blockers and constraints mentioned by participants.
6. Open questions, unresolved disagreements and topics postponed for later.
7. Next steps and follow-up plans explicitly discussed.

Never infer an owner, deadline, commitment or decision from a suggestion alone.
Distinguish proposals and brainstorming from final agreements.
