GENERAL PROFILE

Use this profile when no specific format fits or the format is mixed. Build the
structure from the content instead of forcing it into a lecture, review or meeting
template.

Include, when supported by the material:
1. The main subject, purpose and context.
2. A detailed thematic or chronological account of the content.
3. Important claims, facts, events, examples and explanations.
4. Significant comparisons, disagreements, problems or changes of direction.
5. Conclusions and outcomes explicitly present in the source.
6. Questions raised but left unanswered, only when relevant.

Do not add study questions, action items, a verdict or tutorial steps unless the
source itself calls for them.
