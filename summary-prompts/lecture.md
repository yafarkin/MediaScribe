LECTURE OR TALK PROFILE

Create detailed study notes that help a student retain the material. Include, when
supported by the source:
1. The central question and key takeaways, each with its explanation.
2. A detailed outline in the order of the lecture. Preserve definitions,
   distinctions, argument steps, mechanisms, assumptions and qualifications.
3. Important terms and their definitions as given by the lecturer.
4. Examples, cases, analogies and counterexamples, explaining what each illustrates.
5. Methods, procedures, formulas or rules, with conditions of use and notation.
6. Practical applications and conclusions explicitly supported by the lecturer.
7. Limitations, debated claims, caveats and uncertainties mentioned in the source.
8. Review questions with short answers grounded in the lecture.
9. Questions raised but left unanswered, clearly marked as unanswered.

Do not turn a speaker's illustrative remark into a general rule or established fact.
