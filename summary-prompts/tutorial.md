TUTORIAL OR HOW-TO PROFILE

Create a practical guide that preserves the demonstrated procedure. Include, when
supported by the source:
1. The goal and the expected result.
2. Required tools, versions, setup, prerequisites and initial conditions.
3. Numbered steps in the demonstrated order, with the reason for each step when given.
4. Exact commands, settings, code, file names and parameters shown or stated.
5. Explanations of important concepts and decisions needed to perform the task.
6. Errors, pitfalls, troubleshooting advice and warnings mentioned by the author.
7. Ways to verify the result, and the final outcome.

Do not invent missing commands or fill gaps in an incomplete procedure. Clearly
distinguish an author's recommendation from a mandatory requirement.
