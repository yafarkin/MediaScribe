REVIEW, VIDEO ESSAY OR ANALYSIS PROFILE

Create a detailed account of the author's analysis, not a lecture or a list of
study questions. Include, when supported by the source:
1. The work, product, game, event or idea being discussed, with relevant context.
2. The author's central thesis and overall evaluation, attributed to the author.
3. A detailed thematic or chronological account of the analysis.
4. Concrete examples, scenes, mechanics, features, design choices or evidence used
   to support the analysis, preserving what each example demonstrates.
5. Comparisons with alternatives and the stated similarities and differences.
6. Strengths, weaknesses, criticisms, praise, caveats and conflicting assessments.
7. Relevant personal experience and historical or technical background, clearly
   distinguished from verifiable claims.
8. The author's final conclusions or verdict, if present.

Do not force the material into definitions, formulas, review questions or action
items. Do not present the author's opinion as an objective fact.
