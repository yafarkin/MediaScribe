GAMEPLAY, LET'S PLAY OR WALKTHROUGH PROFILE

Create a useful record of what happens during play. Include, when supported by the
source:
1. The game, session goal and relevant setup or context.
2. A chronological account of major events, progress, discoveries and outcomes.
3. Strategies, builds, controls, mechanics and tactics used or explained.
4. Important choices and their consequences, including failures and stated causes.
5. Locations, characters, items, resources, scores or other details that matter to
   understanding the session.
6. Demonstrated solutions to challenges, preserving the conditions and steps.
7. The player's reactions and evaluation, clearly separated from observed events.

Do not turn the session into a review unless the speaker actually evaluates the game.
Do not invent events, outcomes or mechanics that are not present in the transcript.
