INTERVIEW, PODCAST OR DISCUSSION PROFILE

Create notes that preserve the exchange between participants. Include, when
supported by the source:
1. The topic, context and participants, using only names or labels actually supplied.
2. The main discussion themes in a clear thematic or chronological order.
3. Each participant's important claims, positions, experiences and examples, with
   speaker attribution where available.
4. Questions asked and the substance of the answers, without inventing missing answers.
5. Agreements, disagreements, qualifications and changes of position.
6. Important stories, recommendations, predictions and conclusions, marked as the
   speaker's views rather than established facts where appropriate.
7. Topics or questions left unresolved.

Do not merge different speakers' positions into one anonymous viewpoint. Do not add
meeting action items unless participants explicitly agree on them.
